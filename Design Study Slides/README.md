repository. The slides collect the typography, color, and texture references explored
while choosing the app's look and feel.

## Slides

1. **Design Study**: title slide.
2. **Interface references**: four sources, each labeled with the quality it was chosen for.
   - A personal travel-blog site: bold typography that still gives a sense of calm.
   - A natural-soap shop: a clean feeling, clear fonts, and warm colors.
   - A creative-canvas landing page: big descriptive icons and interesting textures.
   - The Griffy font: a playful, hand-drawn display typeface.
3. **Mood board**: collage-style images (a Tokyo street collage, a cozy checkered
   sticker collage, a *Spirited Away* poster layout, and a grid of retro Japanese
   posters). Each comes with a five-color palette sampled from it, ranging from pinks,
   teal, and orange to sage and yellow-green, mustard, crimson, and cream.

## Takeaways

- Pair bold, expressive headings with calm, clean body text.
- Favor warm colors and soft, textured backgrounds over flat, sterile ones.
- Use large, descriptive icons and imagery so pages are easy to scan.

The reference images belong to their respective creators and are collected here only as
design inspiration.
