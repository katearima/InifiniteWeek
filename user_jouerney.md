## Core User Journey

Keti and Lizi sign up for InfiniteWeek when the app has no accounts or events, just the empty, open MIT Campus group. They both land on Explore, which is also where they start whenever they log in again. Keti creates an approval-only group for the Number Six Club, adds a group picture, and posts a coffee hour with a cover picture and a study session. Lizi finds the group and requests to join. She can see its name and picture, but its events, event covers, and discussions stay hidden until Keti approves her request. Once she joins, Lizi can change the group picture and explore both events. She marks the coffee hour Interested, changes her response to Going after checking her schedule, and saves the study session too. Both events now appear in My Events and Calendar. Keti can also change or remove the coffee hour's cover picture. Lizi asks a question in the discussion and edits her comment to add a detail. Keti replies, and because Lizi is Going, she gets a notification that takes her straight to the highlighted reply. Any further replies stay together in the same thread. As the organizer, Keti can remove comments. If she deletes the first comment in a thread, the app warns her that all its replies and related notifications will be removed too. Later, Keti changes the coffee hour's time or location. Lizi gets an unread notification and opens it to check the new details, which marks it as read. If Keti cancels the event, Lizi gets another notification. The coffee hour stays in her saved events with its cover picture and an Event canceled label, and she can still join the discussion or clear her response. If Lizi leaves the group, her saved responses and notifications for that group are cleared. Her comments remain visible to current members. Rejoining lets her see the discussions again, but it does not bring back her old saved plans or notifications.
