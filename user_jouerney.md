## Core User Journey

Starting with no accounts or events and only the empty, open MIT Campus group, Keti and Lizi sign up for InfiniteWeek and land on Explore; later logins also open Explore. Keti creates the Number Six Club as an approval-only group with a theme picture, publishes a coffee hour with a cover picture and a study session for that group, and approves Lizi's membership request. Before approval, Lizi can discover the group and its picture but cannot read its events, covers, or discussions. After admission, she can change the group's picture, marks the coffee hour `INTERESTED`, then `GOING`, and saves the study session; both appear in My Events and Calendar. Keti can replace or remove her event cover. Lizi asks a question in the coffee-hour discussion and edits her comment; Keti replies beneath that comment, and Lizi receives a Going-only comment alert that opens, focuses, and highlights the exact reply. Replying again stays in the same one-level thread. Keti can moderate comments as the organizer; deleting a root warns that it removes the whole thread and its notifications. When Keti changes the time or location, Lizi receives an unread in-app alert and opens it to see current event details, marking the alert read. If Keti cancels the coffee hour, Lizi receives a cancellation alert, keeps a labeled saved entry with its cover, and can still discuss the event or clear her response. Leaving the group clears Lizi's saved responses and its notification history while retaining her comments for current members; rejoining restores access to comments but does not restore cleared plans or notifications.
