
## User Journey — InfiniteWeek

**Users:** Keti and Tako
**Scenario:** Both users have existing accounts. Some groups and events have already been created.

1. **Keti logs in** to InfiniteWeek and lands on the Explore page, where she can see events from groups she belongs to.
2. **Keti opens Groups** and sees two sections: My Groups, showing groups she has already joined, and Explore Groups, showing groups available to join.
3. **Keti opens My Events**, where she can see events she previously marked as `GOING` or `INTERESTED`.
4. **Keti explores events** and finds the Charles River Sunset Walk. She marks it as `INTERESTED` and leaves a comment.
5. **Tako logs in** using a second account. Since she hasn't joined any groups yet, she opens Explore Groups.
6. **Tako joins MIT Campus** and requests to join Number Six Club and MIT Gala.
7. **Tako opens Explore** and finds the Charles River Sunset Walk. She marks it as `GOING` and replies to Keti's comment.
8. **Keti logs back in**, sees Tako's reply, and responds to her comment.
9. **Keti changes her response** to the Charles River Sunset Walk from `INTERESTED` to `GOING`.
10. **Keti opens Calendar** and sees her upcoming events, including an older event whose date was changed. The app indicates that the event details have been updated.
11. **Keti opens Groups** and sees Tako's pending request to join Number Six Club. She approves the request.
12. **Keti opens an event she previously created** and views the options to edit or cancel it.
13. **Tako returns to her account** and sees a notification about a new comment. She can open or dismiss the notification.
14. **Tako opens Groups** and sees that she is now a member of Number Six Club.
15. **Tako opens the Number Six Club events** and marks the Halloween Party as `GOING`.
16. **Keti cancels the Halloween Party**, and the event's status changes to `CANCELLED`.
17. **Keti opens Calendar** and sees that the Halloween Party is now marked as cancelled.
18. **Tako receives a notification** that the Halloween Party was cancelled.
19. **Tako opens the cancelled event** and sees its updated status.
20. **Tako clears her `GOING` response** to the cancelled event.
21. **Tako opens Calendar** and confirms that the event no longer appears in her personal calendar because she removed her response.
